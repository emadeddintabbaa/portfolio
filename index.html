About

Full mobile products, end to end

Hi everyone — I'm Emad Eddin Tabbaa, a software engineer from Syria who builds complete mobile products. I studied Computer Science and mobile development, and I love shipping apps that are great on the surface and solid underneath. 📱

I don't just build the front-facing app — I build the backend that powers it too: Node.js REST APIs, databases, and real-time infrastructure for things like live multiplayer and chat. Outside of coding, I love playing games and travelling. My goal is simple: strive to build mobile apps that make a difference.
